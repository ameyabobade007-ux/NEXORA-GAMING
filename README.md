## 🕹️ What is NEXORA?

**NEXORA** is a full-stack gaming e-commerce platform designed to combine a modern gaming storefront with a complete business management system.

The platform provides two major experiences:

### 🎮 Customer Experience
Browse games, compare titles, manage wishlists, add games to cart, place orders and track purchases.

### 🛡️ Business Management
Administrators can manage products, inventory, customers, orders, billing and business reports from a dedicated dashboard.

> **NEXORA = Gaming Store + Customer Dashboard + Business Management System**
